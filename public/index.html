Quick Glossary

Learning Track
A structured sequence of courses leading to a specific job role.

Points earned by completing lessons, quizzes, and projects.
Skill Gap
The missing skills between your current level and a target job role.
Gated Progression
You must pass each level to unlock the next one.

A certificate with a unique ID proving you completed all required work.
